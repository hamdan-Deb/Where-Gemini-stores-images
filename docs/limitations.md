# Limitations

## Private infrastructure

The public cannot see every internal service used by Google or Prodia.

## Vendor contracts

Private agreements and subprocessors may not be publicly documented.

## Server-to-server traffic

Browser captures cannot show backend-to-backend traffic that never reaches the client.

## Regional and account differences

Behavior may vary by region, account type, subscription, experiment, or product.

## Documentation changes

AI services change quickly. A finding recorded in September 2026 should be rechecked when the relevant APIs or products change.

## Negative observations

Not seeing Prodia traffic in one test does not prove that Prodia is never used. It only describes that test.

## Architecture inference

A DNS record or HTTP request can provide useful evidence, but it normally does not reveal the full backend architecture.
