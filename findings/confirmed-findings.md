# Confirmed Findings

These are the findings currently supported by public documentation.

1. Google documents the Nano Banana family of image models.
2. Prodia exposes Gemini image models through its API.
3. Prodia documents image-to-image Gemini workflows.
4. Prodia documents one-hour expiry for async job results.
5. Google documents 48-hour deletion for files uploaded through the Gemini Files API.
6. Google publishes material describing Prodia's use of Google Cloud infrastructure.
7. Prodia documents Bearer/JWT authentication for its API.
8. Google documents separate retention controls for Gemini consumer activity.

These findings do not prove that Google consumer Gemini uses Prodia.
