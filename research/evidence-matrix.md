## Evidence labels

**DOCUMENTED**  
Directly stated by an authoritative source.

**OBSERVED**  
Seen during a controlled test.

**CORROBORATED**  
Supported by multiple independent sources.

**INFERRED**  
A technical interpretation that is not directly stated by a source.

**HYPOTHETICAL**  
A possible scenario used for testing or threat modelling.

**UNKNOWN**  
The available evidence cannot answer the question.
